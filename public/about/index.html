About HowToICF

HowToICF publishes construction guides for contractors, GCs, and owner-builders working with insulated concrete forms. The guides cover what you actually need to know before and during a build — concrete mix specs, buck installation, pour sequence, the full project lifecycle.

The content comes from real field experience. Not manufacturer spec sheets. Not code-minimum defaults. If something comes from a specific source, it's cited.

Who's behind it: Eric Kimbriel is a manufacturer's sales rep for Stronghold ICF with 8 years in the field. He works with contractors, GCs, and builders across the Northeast and Southwest US as a single point of contact. Whether ICF is right for your project or not, you'll know right away. Questions on sourcing, specs, or a bid? Please reach out directly.

Questions? Visit the contact page or email eric@strongholdicf.com
