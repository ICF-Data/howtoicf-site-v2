About

Eric Kimbriel, Stronghold ICF sales rep.

8 years as a manufacturer sales rep. Three territories: the Northeast, the Southwest and the Southeast. If you want pricing, a spec check, or a straight read on a first ICF job, start here.

Who I work with

I’m a manufacturer sales rep for Stronghold Insulated Concrete Forms with 8 years’ experience as a manufacturer rep. I’ve always worked with contractors, GCs, builders, do-it-yourselfers, and lumberyards and home centers across the Northeast, the Southwest and the Southeast as your single point of contact. If you’re in the AEC/C community, I work with people like you every day.

Whether ICF is right for your project or not, you’ll know right away. Questions on sourcing, specs, pricing, or putting together a bid for Stronghold ICF — reach out to me directly.

When a project is a fit, I quote Stronghold. FX fixed blocks in 6-inch and 8-inch cores. FD foldable blocks in 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12-inch cores. The block was engineered to deal with blowouts in the form itself, which is the objection I hear most from crews coming off wood or another ICF brand. Full specs, code reports and warranty: strongholdicf.com.

How to work with me

1. Send your plans. PDFs, CAD exports, or phone photos of the drawings.

2. Send your dimensions. Wall lengths, heights, and openings. A hand sketch works.

3. Show me the site. Photos of the lot, grade, and access. Tell me what you’re working with.

4. Get a straight answer. Fit, material takeoff, pricing, and bid support.

Email it all to eric@strongholdicf.com or text 605-269-7898.
