Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy explains what information howtoicf.com collects and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this site (checklist download or pricing request), we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Any project details you provide voluntarily (location, project type, notes)

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

How We Use Your Information

  • Checklist subscribers receive the checklist by email and may receive occasional updates about ICF construction. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Pricing requests are used solely to provide material pricing. We do not add pricing request contacts to a marketing list without your consent.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate this site:

  • Beehiiv — email list management for newsletter subscribers
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Netlify — site hosting and serverless functions

Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your information with other parties.
